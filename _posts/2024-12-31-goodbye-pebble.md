I can't imagine what it must have been like for Pebble losing her brother, they were nearly always together and had gone through so much as a pair with their difficult start in life.  They seemed inseparable, except when Pebble liked going outside a bit more often than he did. I always imagined that she was the one who looked after him at the RSPCA shelter, being the bigger sister to her tiny brother.

Pebble never seemed to ask for anything, as if she had everything she needed in her life.  She was content and happy that she had a loving home.  There were times she would come up to me in the house or garden and have so much to say to me and I'll always wonder what she was talking about – I think she was saying how happy she was.

Apart from being on the small side, Pebble seemed fit and healthy, so agile and energetic, just miniaturised.  I was sure that once she had got past losing Rocket she would have a good long life ahead of her, that she had escaped their past.

Pebble was very upset about losing Jessie.  They weren't close but they respected each other and got along peacefully.  She knew that Jessie had been upset for a little while and then was gone.  She was in a strange house, strange place and now something had happened to her fellow cat.  She understandably withdrew and was upset for a while.  Despite all that she started becoming more brave and friendly again.  Pebble was such a brave girl, but only two weeks after losing Jessie, she became ill and had to be rushed to the vets.  She was only three and her kidneys had failed.  I fussed her for the last time and said goodbye to my pretty little Pebble.

Rocket and Pebble felt like two little angels, arriving at the perfect moment to help me through the worst time of my life.  They were only on this earth for a short while before they were gone.   

Goodbye little Pebble you were such a joy and didn’t let anything get you down.  I thought we had more time together, you deserved it.  I hope one day I will see you and everyone else again so I can tell you again how much I love you.
