I still don't know what to write about little Rocket. It's too painful to believe that my lovely little friend has gone.

Rocket adored me, it was like I could feel his love.  Just being with me made him so happy.  Sitting with me at breakfast, dinner, while I worked at my desk or armchair. He followed me around, including outside, but not too much, he had some independence especially when he wanted a proper sleep.  Like the rest of the gang he would be there waiting when I got home even if it was nowhere near feeding time.  He just wanted to know I was back home.

He was a very sweet boy like his sister, seemingly content and grateful for his life.  Rocket only had two naughty habits, which thankfully he didn't do often; chewing the end of charging cables (which I hid away) and giving Pebble a little nip if he thought he should have me to himself (I would stop him every time).

Like all the gang he was there for me through a terrible time in my life.  It felt like he turned up at the perfect time and was there for me when I needed him the most.  I still can't believe my little friend is no longer here.  I knew with his conditions that he wouldn't be have a long life but he deserved much more.  I'm grateful for every single day he was with me, he was a beautiful friend who I will never forget.

Goodbye baby Rocket, you were the best of friends.  Thank you for being there for me every day when I needed you, you always made me smile.  It felt like we would always be together I still cannot believe you are gone.
