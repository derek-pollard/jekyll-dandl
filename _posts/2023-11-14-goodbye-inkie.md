I’m so proud of Inkie, she obviously missed Cubbie for a long time.  She wasn’t quite the same after losing her adoring big brother.  She stayed reserved after Lindsay passed away and I don’t think she ever stopped missing her. 

She was a good cat, all she really wanted was to know that she was wanted.  At night she often stayed out at bedtime, not just because she liked being outside, but also because she enjoyed me going out to get her.  It showed she hadn’t been forgotten, that I would play chase with her and she could get away with that little bit of cheekiness.  It was her most playful part of the day. 

When Rocket and Pebble first arrived she was not impressed with the new family members, especially as Rocket didn’t seem to understand she didn’t want him to keep saying hello. Over time she mellowed and would even tolerate one of them sitting within a foot or two of her. As long as they didn’t bother her.

Inkie had been doing very well health wise and although she slowed down gradually with age, she remained active and alert.  She loved to spend time outside, even in the cold and the rain as long as she didn’t get wet through the bushes she was hiding under.

I suspected that Inkie was becoming a little unwell and took her to the vets.  A blood test confirmed that she was in the early stages of kidney failure.  I was optimistic that with a special diet she could be comfortable for some time to come.  She took to the new food well and I was very relieved for her.  A few days later her condition deteriorated and I had to take her to the vets.  We got to say goodbye together peacefully, holding her in my arms for the last time.

Goodbye Inkie you were a good girl and friend for many years.
