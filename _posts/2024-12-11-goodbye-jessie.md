Oh Jessie, you were a funny cat.  I was never sure if she was simple or if she had it all so figured out that she knew life was exactly the way she wanted it to be.

For a long time I thought she didn’t like going outside. After a while I realised that she almost always only went out after I’d gone to the garden.  Then she would come back inside when I did, even if she seemed to be having fun and looked relaxed.  The garden was only for when I was out there - how sweet.

Jessie had a special power.  If I took a nap on the bed during the day she would turn up within a minute, often sooner, and lay next to me.  Even if I didn’t know where she was or I thought she was asleep somewhere else, no matter how little noise I thought I made lying down, she would be there.  She loved taking naps with me.

Most people wouldn’t have said Jessie was affectionate as it was random if she would sit on my knee, she wasn’t that bothered about giving or receiving fuss and she was highly concerned at all times about being fed above all else.  It might have seemed that way but I know how much she valued my presence around the house.

Jessie wasn’t like any of the other cats I’ve known.  She was certainly strange with her funny walk, her always trying to trick me into feeding her early and the way she would turn up and just fall on me instead of curling up gently.  I’ll miss her funny and sweet ways, she was a lovely girl.

Right from when she was a kitten Jessie seemed to be the laziest cat I’ve ever known.  She didn’t play for very long, didn’t run around the garden chasing insects and never tried climbing the garden fences.  So I just thought she didn't want to be active.  It turned out after seven years of regular health checks that she had a hard to detect heart defect that only showed up on an ultrasound scan.  Sadly that caught up with her and I lost a dear little friend.  I’m thankful that she lived most of her life happily if a little sedately and her condition never seemed to bother her.

Goodbye Jessie you were a strange but very special girl.
